# Market check: Rewater

Checked 2026-10-15.

**Claim:** fund outdoor water-saving upgrades in homes, verify savings from utility smart-meter data with a
control group, and sell the verified volume to companies with water-positive pledges.

## What exists

| Who | What | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| [Google](https://www.esgdive.com/news/google-commits-to-replenish-more-water-than-it-uses-by-2030/822021/), Microsoft, Amazon, Meta | Committed to be water positive by 2030. Google reported ~7B gallons replenished in 2025 and expects ~19B by 2030; [runs an open RFI](https://datacenters.google/water-rfi/) for replenishment projects. [Microsoft](https://cdn-dynmedia-1.microsoft.com/is/content/microsoftcorp/microsoft/msc/documents/presentations/CSR/Water-replenishment-learnings-on-journey-to-water.pdf) had 49 projects / 61M m³ potential volumetric benefits by July 2023. | The buyers, with budgets and a deadline. |
| Kilimo | Verifies farm irrigation savings and sells them as water credits to companies with pledges. | Precedent for the model, in agriculture. |
| Utility rebate programs (e.g. [MWDOC](https://www.mwdoc.com/save-water/rebates/commercial-rebates/)) | Pay per square foot of turf removed, per controller station. | The channel, and a funding source that could stack with buyer money. |
| [OpenET](https://www.waterworld.com/drinking-water-treatment/press-release/14303958/rigorous-assessment-measures-accuracy-of-openets-evapotranspiration-data) | Satellite ET, validated for farms. Urban accuracy is unproven ([USU](https://extension.usu.edu/cwel/urban-et)). | Why Rewater measures from meters, not satellites. |

## What's open

We found no one selling verified urban/residential outdoor water savings to corporate buyers. The Root-It
interviews found the blocker: most agencies can't measure outdoor savings without smart meters (AMI).

## Risks to check next

- Buyer methodology requirements (VWBA alignment, third-party verification).
- Double counting between utility state targets and buyer replenishment claims.
- Persistence of savings beyond year one.
